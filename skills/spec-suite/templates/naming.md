# Naming — Criteria & Shortlist Format

## Criteria
- Single word.
- Brand-friendly: easy to say, spell, and remember; works as a domain/subdomain.
- Evokes the project's core function or feeling without being generic (avoid "-App", "-Tool", "-Hub" suffixes).
- Check that the word isn't already a heavily-used product name in an adjacent space.

## Shortlist format

Present 5-8 candidates as a short table:

| Name | Why it fits |
|---|---|
| Loom | Weaves two people's timelines together — fits an LDR app |
| ... | ... |

Ask the user to pick one (or narrow to 2-3 favorites) before proceeding to the PRD. Do not generate the PRD or later documents under a placeholder/working title.
